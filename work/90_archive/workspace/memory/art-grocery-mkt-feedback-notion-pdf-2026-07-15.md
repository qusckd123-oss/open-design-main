## Recorded Facts

- Popup period: 2026-07-09 to 2026-07-12, total 4 days.
- Purpose: expand exposure for Art Grocery graphic T-shirt line, drive popup visits and SNS content spread, strengthen brand experience through influencer visit content.
- Content results: pre-content 2, popup content 3, influencer processed content 1, stories including regrams 17, influencer on-site visit content 9.
- Total views: 64,506.
- Costs: popup operation 15,000,000 KRW, influencer 450,000 KRW.
- Sales: 27 units, 1,127,000 KRW.
- Top sellers: cherry graphic T-shirt 10 units; avocado / assemble graphic T-shirts 5 units each; grape graphic T-shirt 4 units.

## Core Interpretation

- Marketing generated awareness, but purchase conversion was weak.
- Cherry performed best because its color point and graphic recognition were clear.
- SNS exposure did not strongly translate into store purchase.
- Main bottlenecks were product impact, price resistance, unclear concept communication, limited participation/photo-zone experience, and weak promotional hooks.

## Issues To Remember

- Product: first impression was weaker than expected versus color/graphic intent; semi-overfit structure lacked freshness; slogan wordplay and story connection were not memorable enough; basic graphic T-shirt perception created price resistance.
- Popup experience: concept was not instantly legible; VM color combination lacked impact; photo zone and participation elements were insufficient; leaflet distribution alone did not communicate the concept.
- Promotion: 2-unit 10% and 3-unit 20% discount did not feel like a strong benefit; no strong limited or hooking purchase device.
- Marketing: content skewed toward visit reviews and did not communicate product appeal enough.

## Next Actions

- Product: strengthen graphic-specific differentiation, color competitiveness, fit variety, product story, and slogan development.
- VM: strengthen popup concept story, add experiential spatial elements, plan photo/content creation zones, add key visual and graphic explanation elements.
- Marketing: expand pre-teasing, styling-centered content, product-selling-point content, short-form Reels, influencer styling content, UGC and SNS participation events.

## Future Popup Checklist

- Explain each graphic's purchase reason in one sentence.
- Make the price acceptable through design, story, rarity, or benefit.
- Make the concept understandable at the entrance within a few seconds.
- Add participation devices such as photo zone, roulette, draw, lucky draw, random goods, or sticker pack.
- Separate content roles: teaser, styling, graphic story, product benefit, UGC, conversion.
