## Decided / Recorded

- The Seongsu Art Grocery popup ran from 2026-07-09 to 2026-07-12.
- Promotion was 10% off for 2 graphic T-shirts and 20% off for 3 graphic T-shirts.
- On-site operation included a promoter at the entrance, product explanation, and leaflet distribution.
- Total four-day sales were recorded as 27PCS.
- Day-one sales on 2026-07-09 were 6PCS, with rain limiting store traffic.
- The front-of-store display attracted attention and helped encourage product touch.
- Colorful postcard-style materials received comparatively positive response.
- Grape and cherry art items received the most touch among grocery products.
- Some customers responded positively to the wordplay graphics such as `LIFE IS GRAPE` and `I DON’T CARROT ABOUT YOU`.
- Foreign customers asked about XL+ sizing.
- Main limitation: without staff explanation, customers had difficulty understanding the popup theme and product-planning intent, so interest did not convert strongly to purchase.
- Follow-up direction: strengthen key visuals, spatial storytelling, and information content so the concept and product story are understandable at a glance.
- After low Seongsu popup sales velocity, Art Grocery products moved to 50% discount operation across flagship stores.
