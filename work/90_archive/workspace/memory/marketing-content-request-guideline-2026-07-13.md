# Marketing Content Request Guideline - 2026-07-13

Source: marketing team guidance shared to planning for future content requests.

## Purpose

Use this as the operating guideline when requesting WACKY WILLY content creation or upload support from the marketing team.

## Request Timing

- Upload request date is not unilateral. Discuss and align upload timing with Park Dasom, the upload/SNS owner.
- Production deadline should not be same-day or one day before if avoidable.
- Normal content production is usually possible if the request is not same-day or one day before, but one week of lead time is preferred.
- For early requests, about two weeks before the desired upload timing is the most appropriate window.
- Requests shared as early as one month in advance are acceptable, but they may get buried under other issues or missed by the owner.

## Prioritization

- Reorder or exclusive status alone does not guarantee immediate content production.
- Marketing schedule can be adjusted based on product quantity, marketing issue priority, and relative importance versus other topics.
- For content upload requests, ask the relevant owner, but final progression must be discussed with the owner.

## Information To Include

When requesting content, provide as much detail as possible so the marketing team can understand the issue and produce/publish content properly.

- Desired upload timing or upload window.
- Inbound date and key sales/launch dates.
- Product type and style numbers.
- Quantity, reorder/exclusive status, and channel/store scope.
- Why the issue matters and what the content should support.
- Any urgency, constraints, or related marketing/online/sales context.
